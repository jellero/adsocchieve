Cycling

Legendary climbs, technical gravel and quiet roads: Carnia is a paradise on two wheels.

Thanks to the Zoncolan and the Crostis, made famous by the Giro d'Italia, Carnia is well known to anyone who loves the great climbs of the Alps. But the whole area rises and falls through breathtaking views and unspoilt landscapes, on a network of paved and gravel roads that leaves you spoilt for choice.

From Socchieve, with the Zoncolan about 24 km away, we suggest two routes: one off-road and one on the road.

The routes
01

The Mediis MTB trail

The best-known mountain-bike route in Carnia, home of the Trofeo Carnia MTB. It crosses the hamlets of the municipality on dirt tracks, alternating short, sharp climbs with technical, demanding descents: ride it with care, and it will reward anyone who loves riding off-road.

Villages: Mediis

02

The Passo Rest loop

A loop linking the upper Pordenone area with Carnia over the Passo del Rest, which can be ridden in either direction: Socchieve, Preone and its valley, San Francesco, Anduins, Vito d'Asio, Clauzetto, Travesio, Meduno, Lake Redona, Tramonti di Sotto and di Sopra, Passo di Monte Rest, Caprizzi, Priuso, Mediis and back to Socchieve. Quiet roads and splendid natural surroundings reward the effort: for experienced cyclists, at least 5 hours.

101 km2,055 m of ascentMax gradient 20%

Villages: Socchieve, Priuso, Mediis
