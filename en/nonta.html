The village

What to see and do in Nonta

Nonta is small and elusive, just as its castle must once have been. The road from Mediis to Socchieve only skirts it and, even along via San Maurizio, the village seems impenetrable: like its lake, it is there but you can't see it. On the other hand, the Osteria di Nonta, said to date back to the 17th century, is famous throughout the area for its traditional Carnic cooking.
