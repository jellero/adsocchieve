Privacy

Version 1.0 · 16 November 2026

This is a translation provided for convenience. In case of any discrepancy, the Italian version prevails.

This notice covers the website of the Albergo Diffuso Socchieve “Col Gentile”, its contact forms and enquiries relating to stays and services. It is provided under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR).

1. Data controller

Zanier Lucio · Albergo Diffuso Socchieve “Col Gentile” · Via Roma 20, Mediis, 33020 Socchieve (UD), Italy · VAT no. 02790180307. Email: info@albergodiffusosocchieve.it · Phone: +39 0433 819934.

2. Data processed

The contact forms may ask for your full name, email address, phone number (optional), message and details of the property or service your enquiry refers to. For bookings and stays we may process identification and contact details, dates, number of guests, preferences, and information needed for the booking and for legal obligations. The servers may log technical access data, including IP address, date, time and resources requested.

3. Purposes and legal bases

Data is used to answer enquiries and prepare quotes (pre-contractual measures), to manage bookings, stays and purchased services (performance of a contract), to comply with tax, administrative and public-security obligations (legal obligation), and to protect the security of the website and prevent abuse or spam (legitimate interest). The forms described here are not used for marketing, unless you receive separate specific information.

4. Bookings, payments and guarantee card

Bookings for the Col Gentile houses go through Slope; bookings for La Farie, the sauna and forest bathing go through Welora. When you use an external booking or payment system, the data you enter there is also processed by the providers involved, according to their respective roles and privacy notices. The website's contact form does not ask for your credit card number. The terms of the guarantee card and of any charges are shown in the booking process and in the booking terms.

5. Recipients and external tools

Where necessary, data may be shared with hosting and email providers, booking and payment services, authorised staff, advisers and, where required by law, the authorities. The website may link to OpenStreetMap maps, 360° tours using external libraries and other third-party content or services. When these resources are loaded, their providers may receive technical connection data.

6. Retention and security

Enquiries are kept for as long as needed to reply and to manage any subsequent relationship; booking and administrative data for the periods required by applicable law. Appropriate technical and organisational measures are in place to protect personal data.

7. International transfers

The use of external services may involve transfers of data outside the European Economic Area. Where they occur, such transfers must take place with the safeguards provided for by the GDPR.

8. Your rights

You can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability or object to processing in the cases provided for by law, and you can withdraw any consent without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing. To exercise your rights, write to info@albergodiffusosocchieve.it. You can lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).

9. Cookies and updates

For information on cookies and integrated services, see the Cookie Policy. This notice may be updated; the current version is published on this page.
