Booking terms for wellness services

Version 1.0 · 16 November 2026

This is a translation provided for convenience. In case of any discrepancy, the Italian version prevails.

These terms apply to sauna and forest bathing bookings offered by the Albergo Diffuso Socchieve “Col Gentile”, Via Roma 20, Mediis, 33020 Socchieve (UD), Italy, VAT no. 02790180307. They form part of the general booking terms.

Prices and duration

The sauna costs 50 € per hour for the whole group, with a minimum of one hour. Forest bathing costs 15 € per person and lasts about two hours. Actual prices, dates and availability are shown before you confirm; the price accepted in the booking applies.

Guarantee card

Online booking may require a guarantee card through Welora. Any charges and applicable financial terms must be shown before you confirm. No additional penalty for late cancellation or no-show is set here unless a specific condition was communicated and accepted in the booking.

Changes, cancellations and no-shows

To change or cancel a booking, contact the property promptly at info@albergodiffusosocchieve.it or on +39 0433 819934. In the event of a no-show, only the penalty conditions communicated before confirmation and accepted for that specific service apply; the published rules consulted state no standard percentage.

How the services take place

Forest bathing takes place outdoors on trails and woodland ground: suitable clothing and footwear are required. For both services, the capacity, times and instructions given in the confirmation apply. For privacy, see the privacy notice.
