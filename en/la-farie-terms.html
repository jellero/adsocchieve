La Farie · Lungis

Terms of stay

These apply to bookings of rooms at La Farie, Via Vincenzo Bellini, Lungis, Socchieve (UD), run by Albergo Diffuso Socchieve “Col Gentile”, Via Roma 20, Mediis, Socchieve, VAT no. 02790180307.

Rates

The available rates are “Room only” and “Bed and breakfast”. The price is the one shown at the time of booking for the chosen dates, room and number of guests.

Cancellation

Free cancellation through the booking portal up to 7 days before arrival, by 18:00. After this deadline, please contact the property.

Arrival and departure

Check-in: from 15:00 to 18:00 · Check-out: from 08:00 to 10:00 · Children: welcome at any age, free cot up to 2 years · Extra bed: on request, 40 € per night · Pets: allowed on request, a supplement may apply · Smoking: not allowed · Parties and events: not allowed.
