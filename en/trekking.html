Trekking

Four loop routes starting right from the villages, through meadows, mule tracks and streams.

Without leaving the villages of the municipality of Socchieve you can enjoy pleasant, healthy walks in a natural and human setting of great tranquillity. We have chosen four routes that can start from the house you are staying in.

For those who want more, there is the Col Gentile crossing on CAI trail 235 from Feltrone to Prato Carnico: 23 km and 1,350 m of ascent, about 7 hours. The reception can arrange a minibus back.

The routes
01

Anello delle Frazioni

From Lungis, follow part of the Nagri trail to the Chialaria meadows and the Frizzon area; along the aqueduct road you reach Feltrone. From the lower part of the village a path leads to Dilignidis; beyond it, just past the church, take the cobbled Pecol path down to Viaso. On the paved road, near a crucifix, turn left onto the Dàs Vàls path and Mediis appears. Heading towards Ampezzo, turn right at the bridge over the Lumiei to the little Borgo Siega and climb back up the Lungjania path to Lungis.

Villages: Lungis, Feltrone, Dilignidis, Viaso, Mediis

02

Anello di Fondas

From Viaso a comfortable mule track descends to the Dusili stream, passes the ruins of the Baldo mill and, heading north, joins the paved road between Feltrone and Enemonzo. You soon reach the terraces of Feltrone; to the east an easy path descends to the Filuvigna stream, beside the farmhouse of the same name. Following MTB route no. 1 you pass a ruined house, and MTB route no. 2 takes you back to Viaso across wide meadows.

Villages: Viaso, Feltrone

03

Flamias

From Viaso a mule track descends south to the SS52 road and the village of Socchieve. Along the main road towards Enemonzo, just before the turn-off for Preone, turn left onto a gravel road and then onto faint paths. You cross a spruce wood and, keeping left, an old lane and a cart track bring you back to the start.

Villages: Viaso, Socchieve

04

Lumiei · Colonia di Nonta

From Nonta, the smallest hamlet, an easy walk follows the mostly dry bed of the Lumiei. Leaving towards Mediis, a cart track on the left leads to the old summer camp where, in the 1930s and 40s, many children spent their summers. You reach Balòt, where after the war there was a carpentry workshop, a mill, a dance hall and a cinema, and then the sports area with a football pitch and children's games. Towards Socchieve, on the opposite bank, you come across fortifications; near the Puart das boras turn left and follow the riverbed back to the start.

Villages: Nonta, Priuso, Socchieve

The route descriptions come from the tourism website of the Municipality of Socchieve. Trails, mule tracks and cart tracks should be walked with care, and conditions may have changed since. The nature you will see is your friend too: respect it.
