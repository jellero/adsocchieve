Getting here

Between the Friulian and Pesarina Dolomites.

Socchieve is in Upper Friuli, in Carnia, along the valley that runs from Villa Santina to Forni di Sotto.

A23 motorwayExit Carnia, then the SS52 towards Tolmezzo and Forni di Sopra (27 km from the exit)
From CadoreSS52 from Forni di Sopra (26 km)
From PordenoneSS552 over Monte Rest from Tramonti di Sopra
From Val d'ArzinoPreone municipal road
By planeTrieste airport (103 km), Venice Marco Polo (about 2 h 30)
By trainCarnia station, then SAF bus (stop in Mediis, Bar alla Posta)
