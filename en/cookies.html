Cookies

This is a translation provided for convenience. In case of any discrepancy, the Italian version prevails.

The website uses technical cookies and tools needed to display its pages, handle enquiries and remember your cookie choice. Only with your consent does it also use Google Analytics to measure visits in aggregate form.

Technical cookies

They are needed for the website to work and do not require consent. Your cookie choice is stored in your browser's local storage (key adsocchieve-consent-v1) until you change it or clear the site data.

Google Analytics (statistics, only with consent)

If you click “Accept” in the banner, the website loads Google Analytics 4, provided by Google Ireland Limited, to count visits and page views and understand how the site is used. The cookies _ga and _ga_* are set, lasting up to 2 years. Google processes the data as a processor and may transfer it to the United States with the safeguards provided for by the GDPR. We do not use Google Analytics for advertising or profiling. If you reject, Google Analytics is not loaded.

You can change your mind at any time from the Cookie preferences link at the bottom of every page.

External services

The Slope and Welora booking systems may use their own cookies and technical tools within their environments or widgets. Other external components, such as the OpenStreetMap map and the virtual tour, may receive technical data when they are loaded. See the notices provided by these providers for the categories, purposes and duration of their cookies and how to manage your preferences.

Preferences and contact

You can manage or delete cookies in your browser settings and, where available, through the controls in the external services. For requests about personal data, see the privacy notice or write to info@albergodiffusosocchieve.it.
