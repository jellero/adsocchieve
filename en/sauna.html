Wellness in Socchieve · Carnia

How the sauna works

After a hike, a day on the bike or on the slopes of Forni di Sopra, the sauna is the best way to loosen your muscles and slow down.

It is booked by the hour for the whole group: the price is for the booking, not per person. One hour minimum, then you decide how long to stay.
