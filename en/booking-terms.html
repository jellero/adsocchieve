Booking terms

Version 1.0 · 16 November 2026

This is a translation provided for convenience. In case of any discrepancy, the Italian version prevails.

These terms cover stays at the Albergo Diffuso Socchieve “Col Gentile”, the La Farie bed & breakfast and the sauna and forest bathing services. Operator: Zanier Lucio, Albergo Diffuso Socchieve “Col Gentile”, Via Roma 20, Mediis, 33020 Socchieve (UD), Italy, VAT no. 02790180307 · info@albergodiffusosocchieve.it.

1. Prices, availability and confirmation

Availability, total price, any supplements, deposit, rate conditions and payment methods are shown before the booking is confirmed or stated in the accepted quote. The booking is confirmed on the terms set out in the confirmation sent to the guest. Specific rate conditions expressly communicated and accepted before confirmation take precedence over the general provisions on this page.

2. Stays in the Col Gentile houses

According to the rules on the official website of the Albergo Diffuso Socchieve, the price includes use of the accommodation and bed and bath linen. The accommodation is available from 12:00 and must be vacated by 10:00 on the day of departure, unless otherwise agreed or stated in the confirmation. Payment of the balance is preferably made on arrival; any extras are settled on departure.

Supplements and reductions for the Col Gentile houses

The official rules list the following supplements: pets, where allowed, 5 € one-off; baby cot, 5 € one-off; extra cleaning, 10 € for small apartments or 15 € for large apartments; extra bed linen, 4 €; extra towel, 1 € each. For stays of fewer than three nights they state a 30% surcharge, and for single use of an apartment a 50% increase on the rate. The reductions stated are: free up to age 3, 50% from 4 to 6 and 30% from 7 to 10. The actual amounts and conditions of the booking are those confirmed in the quote or rate summary before purchase.

3. Deposit, cancellation and early departure in the Col Gentile houses

The published rules state that, in case of cancellation, any deposit paid is not refunded; by agreement with the management it may be used for another date on equivalent terms. For early departure, the cost of the accommodation alone is charged for the unused period, unless otherwise agreed. If the booked rate has a different cancellation policy, the one communicated and accepted during booking applies.

4. La Farie

For the La Farie bed & breakfast, the condition stated on the booking page is: free cancellation through the portal up to 7 days before arrival, by 18:00. For later cancellations, no-shows, any deposits or charges, only the amounts and conditions expressly shown and accepted during booking apply; this page introduces no further penalty percentage.

5. No-shows and guarantee card

If the guest does not arrive on the agreed date and time and has not cancelled within the deadline, the financial conditions of the specific booking apply. For Col Gentile, the rule on any deposit paid continues to apply, unless a different rate was agreed. No additional standard percentage penalty for no-shows is published: further charges to the guarantee card may only be made if the amount or the method of calculating the penalty was communicated before confirmation and accepted by the guest. Where required, the guarantee card is requested and handled within the booking system.

6. Sauna and forest bathing

The sauna costs 50 € per hour for the group; forest bathing 15 € per person. Online bookings may require a guarantee card through Welora. Changes, cancellations and no-shows are governed by the specific conditions stated and accepted during booking. Unless a penalty is explicitly communicated before confirmation, this page introduces none. See also the detailed wellness terms.

7. House rules

Every guest, including minors, must present a valid identity document for legal requirements. Unregistered persons may not stay overnight. Smoking is not allowed in the accommodation. Please respect quiet hours from 23:00 to 08:00 and from 14:00 to 16:00. The rules prohibit taking the property's linen outside the apartments and using appliances to cook or heat food in rooms that are not kitchens. Missing linen, damage or lost keys may be charged according to the actual damage and applicable law.

8. Assistance and privacy

For cancellations, changes or questions, contact info@albergodiffusosocchieve.it or +39 0433 819934. Personal data is processed in accordance with the privacy notice.
