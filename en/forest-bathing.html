Forest bathing

Wellness in Socchieve · Carnia

What is forest bathing

Forest bathing, known in Japanese as Shinrin-yoku, is a wellbeing practice based on slow, mindful and sensory immersion in the forest, to restore body and mind.

With Petra you walk and pause in the woods around the villages of Socchieve: an experience that helps you slow down, step away from the rush and reawaken your senses through deep contact with nature. You spend about two hours together, in small groups.

What to bring

How to prepare

Layered clothingcomfortable and breathable, suitable for outdoors
Waterproof or windproof jacketfor changes in the weather
Hiking boots or trainersto walk safely on the forest floor
Water bottleto stay hydrated
Blanket or small waterproof matto sit or lie on the ground
Insect repellentif you like
A light snack
Notebook and penoptional, to jot down thoughts and feelings

To get the most out of forest bathing, your phone stays on silent or airplane mode during the practice: let anyone who needs to know that you will be unreachable for a couple of hours.
