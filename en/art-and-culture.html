Art and culture

Romanesque churches, frescoes and a festival that every summer tells the story of Carnia through the hands of its craftspeople.

The municipality of Socchieve holds some of the most interesting artistic gems of Carnia, such as the Church of San Martino and the Pieve di Castoia. But it is not just about the “immovable” heritage: this is where Carnia's most important festival of Art, Culture and Work takes place, with craftspeople and artists from Carnia and Friuli whose works tell the story of a land that perhaps speaks better with its hands than with words.

Art and culture

Church of San Martino

In the centre of the village, with its back to the Castoia hill, the little church of San Martino is one of the most typically Carnic religious buildings: an entrance porch, a bell gable, a timber-trussed roof and a covering of curved tiles.

In the sacristy and at the base of the small apse, remains of high-quality Romanesque frescoes have come to light (Christ in a mandorla with the symbols of the Evangelists, fragments of Apostles' heads, decorations), testifying to the building's age.

Art and culture

Rassegna Carnica Arte Cultura Lavoro

Organised by the Comitato Gianfrancesco da Tolmezzo, this summer festival gives space to the culture, art and work that Carnia and Friuli have to offer: a chance to meet and share ideas, keeping attention on the small but important things of mountain life.
