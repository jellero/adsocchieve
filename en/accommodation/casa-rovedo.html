Casa Rovedo

Feltrone, SocchieveUp to 5 guests3 stars
House with garden in Feltrone · Carnia

Detached three-storey house with a large private garden and terraces over the valley, in Feltrone.

Casa Rovedo is in Feltrone, the highest hamlet of Socchieve, at the end of an old row of houses. Recently renovated, it spreads over three floors and sits in a large private garden; each floor has its own terrace overlooking the valley.

Two bedrooms, a kitchen and a bathroom, furnished in a simple, practical style. The property has two separate entrances.
